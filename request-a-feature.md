Have an idea for Horizon?

I'd love to hear what would make Horizon more useful for you. Whether it's a new feature, a workflow improvement, or something that would make planning easier, your feedback helps shape future versions.

## Before you suggest something

Horizon is intentionally built around a few principles:

- No required accounts, no required cloud sync, no central servers
- No selling or sharing of your financial data
- No unnecessary complexity
- Long-term projection over day-to-day budgeting
- A balance you recorded is a fact, and is never overwritten by a projection

Some ideas may not fit those principles, but I read every suggestion.

## What to include

The most helpful requests include:

- What you'd like Horizon to do
- Why it would be useful to you
- How you currently handle this today (if applicable)
