Hi, I'm Bob Larson, a data nerd and privacy geek from Minnesota.

I built [Runway](https://rglarson13.github.io/runway-site/) to answer a short-term question: **"Do I have enough in checking to cover my upcoming bills?"**

Horizon answers the long-term one: **"If I keep doing what I'm doing, where does this end up?"**

I had that plan in a spreadsheet for years -- accounts, contributions, an employer match, a growth rate, one row per year. It worked, but it couldn't answer the questions I actually had. What happens if I stop contributing to the kids' college funds in six years? What if I retire two years earlier? Which of these accounts runs dry first? Every one of those meant rebuilding the sheet.

So I built a tool that models it properly, and keeps the same promise Runway does: your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no bank connections, no tracking.

If you have ideas, questions, or find a bug, I'd love to hear from you!
