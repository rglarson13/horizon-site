## Where does this all end up?

Horizon answers one question:

> "If I keep doing what I'm doing, what am I worth in twenty years?"

Every account, contribution, employer match, and growth assumption is projected forward -- so the plan you have in your head becomes a number you can check.

## Built differently

Horizon is a net-worth projection app, not a budgeting app.

No transactions to categorize.
No spending to track.
No linking your bank.

Horizon is also privacy-first.

No account required.
No cloud sync required.
No collecting your personal information.

Your financial data stays in your hands.

## Features

- Project every account forward, year by year
- See growth and contributions broken out separately, so you can tell when compounding starts to outpace what you put in
- Model contributions that change over time -- step up, wind down, or stop
- Real employer matches, capped against the salary you choose
- Group accounts into goals, and plan what you'll spend from them
- Get warned when a goal can't fund its own plan
- Compare scenarios side by side without touching your real numbers
- Track assets and debts together -- house, mortgage, car, student loan
- Export your data whenever you want

## Recorded, or projected

Horizon keeps a firm line between the two. A balance you have actually recorded is a fact and is never overwritten by a projection. Everything else is derived from those facts, live -- so changing a growth assumption changes the forecast and nothing else.

That distinction is why a projection can be trusted: nothing in the app quietly turns a guess into history.
